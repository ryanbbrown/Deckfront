# Direct Force

## Purchases

Prefer Press, Drive, and Breaker. Buy Silver when no useful force card is affordable. Buy Gold when it does not reduce tactical card density too far.

## Board priorities

Take every available ring-out. Keep one piece in contact after a push. Build repeated displacement lines before the opponent can escape.

## Risk and tie breakers

Accept an exposed piece when the action scores or creates a forced scoring line. Prefer the line that keeps both pieces useful in the next round.
