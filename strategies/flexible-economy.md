# Flexible Economy

## Purchases

Prefer Silver early and Gold when affordable. Buy the strongest tactical card that complements the current hand and discard.

## Board priorities

Take every available ring-out. Preserve central access while the deck develops. Use baseline movement to reduce reliance on movement cards.

## Risk and tie breakers

Avoid purchases that leave fewer than four tactical cards in the deck. Prefer reliable future buying power when board choices score equally.
