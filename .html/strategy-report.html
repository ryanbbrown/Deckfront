How to read the heatmaps

  • Metagame: optimal-play representation from equilibrium strategy weights.
  • Pair cell: both, row only, column only, and neither add to 100%. The colored strip shows the same four outcomes.
  • Pair detail: select a cell to see the card names and exact breakdown. The denominator is K eligible kingdoms, not a raw strategy count.
