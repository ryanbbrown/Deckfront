How to read this report

  • Lottery strategy: at least 0.1% of the final equilibrium after smaller weights are removed and the remaining weights are normalized.
  • Near-50% strategy: less than 0.1% lottery weight and at least 48% score against the material lottery. This two-point band is a provisional screen, not proof of equal strength.
  • Viable strategy: either a lottery strategy or a near-50% strategy.
  • Final lottery: the hardest discovered mix for one strategy to beat. The report scores all discovered strategies against the material part of that mix.
  • Plan use: a card in the starting build, finite purchase plan, or repeat purchase. Acquired use is separate and comes from evaluated games.

Four normal curated kingdoms are an initial sample. They cannot establish card health across a broad kingdom corpus.
