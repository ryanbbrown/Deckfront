Reading and caveats

The retune did what it was aimed at: the pure-Mage equilibrium share across these kingdoms fell from 79.8% to 17.9%, mana-family damage fell from 65.3% to 20.6%, and the changed spells largely left the metagame (3 of 8 kingdoms now acquire none of them). Diversity improved: single-strategy kingdoms fell from 7 to 2 and mean effective size rose from 1.25 to 1.94. Two kingdoms to watch: 005 is still 100% Mage with 71% mana damage (Starfire remains bought there), and 090 swung from a three-strategy mix to 100% Melee. Different evaluation seeds can produce different matrices and lotteries, so treat per-kingdom flips as directional; cross-kingdom aggregates are the signal.
