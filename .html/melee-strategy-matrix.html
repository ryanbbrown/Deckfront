Card effects

  • Drive: At Close range, deal 2 damage. Then move both fighters 1 space left or right so they remain Close. If a wall blocks the move, neither fighter moves, and the attack deals 2 additional damage.
  • Heavy Blow: At Close range, deal 4 damage.
  • Flurry: At Close range, deal 1 damage for each other Tactical Action played this turn, to a maximum of 5.
  • Feint: At Close range, the next Close-range attack this turn deals 2 additional damage.
